Privacy Policy — Exam Memorizer

Last updated: October 2026 (version 1.1.0)
Developer: dev-himchita | Package: com.himadri.exam_memorizer

Overview

Exam Memorizer is a study app for memorizing exam material with cards, topics and progress tracking. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what is shared with third parties. The app has no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDKs, and the developer runs no servers.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

Exam Memorizer does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on our own servers:

  • Your name, email address, or account credentials
  • Your location
  • Your contacts or call logs
  • Keystrokes or clipboard content
  • Browsing history inside or outside this app
  • The content of your exams, cards or study notes

2. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

All of the following is stored only on your device in a local database and app storage. It never leaves your device except as described in Section 5.

  • Exams, topics and cards — everything you create or import
  • Study progress — XP, badges, streaks and unfinished sessions
  • Reading library and highlight cards
  • App preferences — theme, view settings and the storage location you choose
  • Backup files — database files you export to a location you choose

3. Permissions Used and Why

Permission Why it is needed
Internet Used only for the optional Google Drive backup (Section 5). The app makes no other network requests
Read External Storage (Android ≤ 12) Import a database backup file you pick on older Android versions
Write External Storage (Android ≤ 12) Save backup files on older Android versions
All Files Access (Android 11+) Lets the app read and write the database file in a folder you pick (for example Downloads) for backup, restore and the storage-location setting. It is requested only when you use these features, and the app does not browse or read your other files

Note: No permission is used to track you or build advertising profiles.

4. Screen Wake Lock

During study sessions the app may keep your screen awake so it does not turn off while you read. No data is collected by this.

5. Google Drive Backup (Optional)

The Back up to Google Drive feature is optional and is off until you sign in with a Google account. When you do:

  • The app asks for one permission only: access to its own private app-data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). It cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.
  • When you tap Back up to Google Drive, a copy of the app database is uploaded to that folder, replacing the previous backup.
  • When you tap Restore from Google Drive, that backup is downloaded to your device and, after your confirmation, replaces the app's data.
  • Your Google account email is shown inside the app only so you can see which account is connected.
  • The backup lives in your own Google account. The developer cannot access it, and the app sends nothing to the developer or any other third party.
  • The backup counts toward your Google Drive storage.

Sign-in is handled by Google's own sign-in service (Google Play Services). Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can sign out in the app at any time. To remove the app's access, visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the backup, use Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps and delete the app's hidden data.

6. Backup & Restore Files

The Export database file feature creates a copy of your local database and shares or saves it where you choose. Import database file replaces the app's data with a file you pick. These files:

  • Contain a plain SQLite database, which is not encrypted
  • Are never uploaded by this app anywhere unless you use the Google Drive backup or choose a destination in the share sheet
  • Are stored only where you put them, so protect that location if you consider the content sensitive

7. Sharing Features

When you export or share a file, the app passes it to your device's standard share sheet. It is sent only to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Gmail), and the app itself does not upload it.

8. Advertising, Analytics and Tracking

Exam Memorizer contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not read or use your Advertising ID.

9. Children's Privacy

Exam Memorizer is not directed at children. The app collects no personal information from anyone. If you have a concern about a user's age, contact us at dev.himchita@gmail.com.

10. Data Security

Layer Protection
Local database Plain SQLite stored in app private storage, inaccessible to other apps without root
Backup files Unencrypted database file, stored only where you choose; protect that location if needed
Network transmission The only network traffic is the Google Drive backup, sent over HTTPS to Google

We do not operate any servers that store your personal data or study content.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as new features are added. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Data Deletion

All exams, cards, progress and settings are stored locally on your device only. You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app — removes all local data permanently
  • Clearing app data — Settings → Apps → Exam Memorizer → Storage → Clear Data

Google Drive backups are stored in your own Google account and can be deleted as described in Section 5. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the developer at dev.himchita@gmail.com.

This privacy policy covers the Android version of Exam Memorizer (com.himadri.exam_memorizer).
